Objectives:

1. Review the games listed on this page:
   https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_video_games_listed_among_the_best

2. Identify the recurring design patterns that make these games successful. Focus on:
   - core gameplay loops
   - player motivation and progression
   - emotional engagement
   - replayability
   - social, competitive, or cooperative features
   - innovation in mechanics, narrative, or immersion

3. Propose 3 original game concepts inspired by the strongest ideas from those games.
   Each concept should combine mechanics or design principles from at least 5 existing games, but it must still feel new rather than copied.

4. Select the single most innovative concept and expand it into a polished game pitch.

Output Requirements:

- Start with a short summary of the most common strengths found across the reviewed games.
- Present 3 game concepts with:
  - title
  - genre
  - platform
  - target audience
  - core gameplay loop
  - key innovation
  - which existing games influenced it
- Then choose the best concept and provide:
  - a one-paragraph elevator pitch
  - a detailed gameplay overview
  - progression and reward systems
  - multiplayer or social features, if relevant
  - art style and tone
  - why this idea is innovative in today's market

Constraints:

- Do not simply clone existing games.
- Combine only the strongest compatible ideas.
- Prioritize originality, commercial potential, and player engagement.
- Keep the concept realistic enough to be developed by a modern indie or mid-sized studio unless otherwise stated.

Goal:

Create a game idea that feels fresh, compelling, and highly marketable by learning from the best games ever made and recombining their strengths in a new way.
